### Meet Amit Tandon



### India's Favorite Family Comedian


Amit Tandon has made over 200 million people laugh on YouTube alone. With 2000+ live shows across 30 countries, he's one of India's most successful stand-up comedians—and he's just getting started.

His Netflix special "Family Tandoncies" made history as the first Hindi stand-up solo on the platform, staying in India's Top 10 for 15 days. Whether he's talking about marriage, parenting, or the quirks of everyday life, Amit's clean, relatable humor brings families together.

Beyond the stage, Amit has hosted the late-night show "Good Night India" on SonySAB for 126 episodes, created the hit Spotify Original podcast "Andar ki Baat," and even commentated on IPL 2021 with Hotstar. From TV to streaming to podcasts, he's bringing laughter everywhere.
