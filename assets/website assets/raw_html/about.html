About Amit Tandon

From engineering graduate to one of India's most successful stand-up comedians, Amit Tandon has built a career on making millions laugh with his relatable observations about marriage, parenting, and everyday life.

The Journey

Amit Tandon is one of India's highest-selling stand-up comedians, with over 200 million views on YouTube alone. His Netflix special "Family Tandoncies" made history as the first Hindi stand-up solo special on the platform and remained in India's Top 10 for 15 days—the only Indian stand-up solo show to achieve this milestone.

With more than 2000 shows performed across 30 countries, Amit has established himself as one of India's best-known observational comics, bringing clean, family-friendly humor to audiences worldwide.

Comedy Philosophy

Known for his clean, observational humor, Amit focuses on universal themes that bring families together. His jokes about marriage, parenting, cultural quirks, and everyday life resonate across generations and geographies—creating moments of shared laughter that transcend boundaries.

Whether performing in Mumbai or Melbourne, his ability to find humor in the mundane makes every show feel personal and relatable.
