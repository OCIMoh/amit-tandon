Last Updated: June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how tandonamit.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help improve functionality, performance, and user experience.

2. Types of Cookies We May Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the operation and security of the Website.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand:

  • Visitor behavior
  • Website performance
  • Popular content
  • User engagement

Examples may include services such as Google Analytics.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember user preferences and settings to improve browsing experience.

Marketing Cookies

Where applicable, cookies may be used to measure advertising effectiveness and audience engagement.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party service providers may place cookies through the Website, including:

  • Analytics providers
  • Embedded content providers
  • Social media platforms
  • Advertising and marketing platforms

Their use of cookies is governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow users to:

  • Accept cookies
  • Reject cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Configure cookie preferences

Disabling cookies may affect certain Website features and functionality.

5. Consent

By continuing to use the Website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this Policy, subject to your browser settings and applicable law.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
