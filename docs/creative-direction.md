# Amit Tandon website POC — Creative direction (LOCKED)

**Status:** Locked for POC · **Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Subject:** Amit Tandon  
**Concept (internal):** Common Sense, Under Review  
**Tagline idea:** A website about the questionable logic of ordinary life.

## Core insight
Amit finds humour in the logic people apply to everyday situations, including their own behaviour. *Aise Kaise* explores changing times and rituals, and questions the logic behind how friends, family, and Amit himself behave.

## Central idea
People have explanations for everything. The more closely you examine those explanations, the more contradictions you find.

## Visual expression
Highly organised editorial design deliberately interrupted by contradictory statements, annotations, unexpected compositions, and real moments from Amit's comedy.

## Purpose
Intelligent, observant, witty, and unmistakably connected to Amit's perspective — not only to the fact that he is a comedian.

## Final decisions

| Element | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Creative concept | Common Sense, Under Review |
| Core idea | Gap between how people think life works and how they actually behave |
| Design style | Experimental editorial, strong typographic identity |
| Colour | Warm off-white, ink black, restrained vermilion red |
| Typography | Oversized expressive headlines + clean highly legible sans |
| Imagery | Real photographs of Amit, authentic performance stills, genuine comedy footage |
| Interaction | Deliberate interruptions, expressive transitions, editorial reveals |
| Business priority | Upcoming shows and ticket purchase immediately accessible |
| Current show | Feature *Aise Kaise* with verified dates and ticket links |

## Visitor experience (page arc)
1. **Opening — an observation** — Bold type + portrait interrupt + live-show CTA  
2. **Material — contradictions** — Real clips/specials with real titles and links  
3. **Live — Aise Kaise?** — Bold visual shift; cities, dates, venues, tickets (mobile-first booking)  
4. **Person — Amit Tandon** — Restrained story, specials, other work (not a résumé dump)

## Explicitly NOT doing
No literal courtroom, police dossier, engineering dashboard, or laboratory metaphors.  
No family living-room central metaphor, generic mic photography, neon comedy-club look, repetitive card grids, fake handwritten jokes, or AI-generated portraits of Amit.

## Next deliverable
Single high-fidelity full-page website mockup (nav → hero → comedy → live shows → bio → footer), judged on Identity, Art direction, and Usability.
