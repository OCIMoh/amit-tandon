04 · The person

Amit Tandon

A quieter close — credibility without a résumé wall.

Production should replace this with approved performance stills. Public promotional imagery only for this mockup.

Observational comedy that families actually sit through together — specific, clean, and rooted in how people behave, including Amit himself.

He has taken that voice to Netflix with Family Tandoncies, the first Hindi stand-up solo on the platform; to television with Good Night India on Sony SAB; to podcasting with Andar ki Baat; and to stages across more than thirty countries.

The website keeps the career facts, but it does not turn them into a scoreboard. The point is the perspective, not the tally.
