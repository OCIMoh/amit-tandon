# Biography facts for the Person section

## Usable short bio direction
Amit Tandon is an observational Hindi stand-up comedian. His humour comes from the logic people apply to ordinary life — marriage, parenting, friends, and his own contradictions. That is the same insight behind the locked creative direction *Common Sense, Under Review*, and behind his current touring show *Aise Kaise*.
